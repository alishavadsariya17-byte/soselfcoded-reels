# Song bank: real songs Alisha adds in the Instagram app

Pick 3 songs for each draft that match the feeling of the words: one well-known movie song, one movie score or
instrumental, and one wildcard (Bollywood or a currently trending sound). Say which part to use (usually the
chorus or the most emotional moment) and when it should hit, for example "the highlight swipe". Song
availability in Instagram's library varies by country and account, so always give 3 options. Don't repeat the
same song within a week; check `state.json.posted[].songs`. Add songs that worked (see insights) and any she
mentions.
